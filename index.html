Lady Barbeque

Coming from a noble family, Lady Barbeque is the daughter of a rich merchant, and thus invited to the Grand Masquerade Ball at the Palace. Lady Barbeque loves to wear pink and is a great leader and an equally great friend.


Lady barbeque loves pink so much that she has her whole room in different shades of pink color. Her bedsheets are pink, her sofas are pink, her table is pink, her book covers are also pink, and of course she also have a pink bookmark. She loves to read, and all her book covers are made pink only for her.


She likes to play dress-up games with her friends, and they like to prank people together too. But sometimes, they actually have a mission to complete. We don't know what the mission is because it is a secret mission, and they cannot tell people about it. So lets not talk about it either.
